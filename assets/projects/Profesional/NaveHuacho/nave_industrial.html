INDUSTRIAL WAREHOUSE

Preliminary Design · Huacho, Peru

2025 Huacho, Peru 24m span

Project Overview

Complete structural design of a 25-meter clear-span industrial warehouse for vehicle storage. The project includes the design of main portal frames, crane runway beams for a 10-ton bridge crane, bracing systems, and connections.

Design Parameters

  • Clear span: 25m
  • Height: 10m at eaves
  • Frame spacing: 6m
  • Roof cladding: Aluzinc TR4
  • Wind speed: 75 km/h
  • Seismic zone: Zone 4 (Peru)

Software Used

  • SAP2000 - Structural analysis
  • IDEA StatiCa - Connections
  • Revit / Tekla - Modeling & detailing
  • AutoCAD - Drawings

Key Results

  • Maximum drifts: H/400
  • Connection types: bolted & welded
  • Complete calculation report
