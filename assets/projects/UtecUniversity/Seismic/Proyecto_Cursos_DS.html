EARTHQUAKE RESISTANT THEATER

Reinforced Concrete Design · Carabaya, Puno · 5 Stories

Course: Earthquake Resistant Engineering

Project Summary

Structural design of a reinforced concrete theater in a seismic zone (Puno). Three structural systems were evaluated (moment frame, dual system, structural walls), selecting the structural wall system for its superior drift control. Analysis according to Peruvian Standards E.020, E.030, E.060 and ACI 318-14 using ETABS software.
