STEEL INDUSTRIAL WAREHOUSE

Solid Web vs Truss Systems · Lurigancho-Chosica, Lima

Course: Steel Structures Design

Project Summary

Structural design of a large-span industrial warehouse (85×35 m, 12 m height) located in Chosica. Two proposals were developed: solid web beams (rolled/welded profiles) and truss system with SCBF bracing. Analysis according to Peruvian Standards E.020, E.030, E.090 and AISC 360-22. Includes reports, drawings, ETABS and Tekla models.

Proposal: Solid Web

  • Drifts: 0.90% (X) · 0.677% (Y) · Compliant
  • ✓ Shear Design, LTB, Deflection

Proposal: Truss (SCBF)

  • Purlins: W6x16 · Deflection L/300
  • Trusses: Chords HSS6x6x1/2 · Verticals HSS2x2x3/16
  • Lattice: W5x19 (top) + HSS6x6x1/2 (bottom) + HSS2x2 internal
  • Composite Column: HSS8x8x1/2 + HSS12x12x1
  • Controlled Drifts: 0.84% X / 0.008% Y
